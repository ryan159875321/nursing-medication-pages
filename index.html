Medication calculation revision

Quick, mobile-first practice for nursing medication calculations.

Nursing Medication helps learners practise calculation-style questions, review mock test performance, and identify topics that need more revision.

Get support Read privacy policy

Education only

Nursing Medication is a revision and education app. It is not a prescribing, dispensing, administration, medicines management, or clinical decision support tool. It must not be used as a source of truth for real patient care.

Practice

Topic practice and mock tests cover medicine calculation scenarios such as tablets, liquids, injections, infusions, conversions, and weight-based dosing.

Progress

Registered users can save mock results so they can review previous scores and revisit weaker areas.
