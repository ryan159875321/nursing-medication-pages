Who this policy is for

This policy explains how MedMath handles information for people using the app for medication calculation revision and education.

Data we collect

Guest users can use the app without creating an account.

Registered users may provide or generate:

  • Name, if requested in the app
  • Email address
  • Authentication details handled by the authentication provider
  • Mock test scores and answers
  • Topic-level revision performance
  • Basic technical information needed for app reliability

How we use data

  • To let users register and sign in
  • To save progress across sessions and devices
  • To show mock test results and revision focus areas
  • To investigate support requests
  • To improve app reliability and the learning experience

Guest use

Guest progress is stored on the device. If the app is deleted, the device is changed, or local app data is cleared, guest progress may be lost.

Registered accounts

Registered account data is stored using Supabase services. Supabase provides authentication and database services for account-backed progress.

Medical disclaimer

MedMath is for education and revision only. It is not a prescribing, dispensing, administration, medicines management, or clinical decision support tool.

Data deletion

Users can request account and progress deletion by contacting: support@medmathtrainer.com.

Contact

For privacy questions, contact: support@medmathtrainer.com.
