Clinical Disclaimer

Education and revision only

Nursing Medication is a revision and education app. It is not intended to diagnose, treat, prescribe, dispense, verify, or administer medicines.

Not a source of truth for patient care

The app must not be used as a source of truth for real patient care, prescribing, dispensing, medicines administration, or medicines management decisions.

Local policies and professional standards

Users must follow local medicines policies, employer procedures, supervision arrangements, training requirements, and professional standards.

Content review
