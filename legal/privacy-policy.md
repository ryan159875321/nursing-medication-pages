# Privacy Policy

Last updated: 11 May 2026

MedMath is a medication calculation revision app.

## Data We Collect

Guest users can use the app without creating an account. Guest progress is stored on the device.

Registered users may provide:

- Name
- Email address
- Authentication details handled by the authentication provider
- Mock test scores
- Topic-level revision performance

## How We Use Data

We use this data to:

- Let users sign in
- Save progress across sessions
- Show mock test scores and revision focus areas
- Improve app reliability and learning experience

## Medical Disclaimer

The app is for education and revision only. It is not a prescribing, dispensing, administration, or clinical decision support tool.

## Data Deletion

Users can request account and progress deletion by contacting: replace-before-launch@example.com.
